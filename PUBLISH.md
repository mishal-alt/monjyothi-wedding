# Getting your website live (no coding needed)

Your finished website is already built and sitting in the `dist` folder inside this project folder on your Mac:

`/Users/pro/Desktop/work/ZT/monjyothi wedding w2/dist`

## Get a live link in under a minute

1. Open a web browser and go to **netlify.com/drop**
2. Open Finder and go to the `dist` folder above
3. Drag the whole `dist` folder from Finder onto that Netlify Drop page
4. Netlify gives you a working link immediately (something like `random-name.netlify.app`) — that's your live wedding website, ready to share on WhatsApp

## Making the link permanent (optional, still no coding)

The link from step 4 works right away, but it can disappear if you don't save it. To make it permanent and free forever:

1. On that same Netlify page, click "Sign up" (email or Google sign-in)
2. Your site gets claimed to your account automatically
3. You can rename the link (Site settings → Change site name) to something like `mridula-monjyoti.netlify.app`

## Using your own domain name (optional, whenever you're ready)

If you later buy a domain (e.g. `mridulaandmonjyoti.com` from GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.):

1. In Netlify: Site settings → Domain management → Add a domain
2. Netlify shows you 1–2 DNS records to add at wherever you bought the domain
3. Your domain provider's site has a simple form to paste those in (no coding — just copy/paste)
